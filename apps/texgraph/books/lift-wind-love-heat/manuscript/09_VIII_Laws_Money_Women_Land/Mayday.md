# Mayday

I rode out at the turn of May,  
when green the hedges twist and vine;  
a red laced girl was in my way  
Her sash she tied to crossing sign.

"Unknot me, sir, if you are bold,  
but mark: this braid’s a binding thread.  
It loops the vows that once I told  
one knot for each sweet word I said."

I passed her by and did not choose  
but still that thread is on my shoulder.  
The knot I dared not touch, I lose  
and May rides back each year, no older.
