# IV. The Gods Touch Flesh

He serves the fields, and serves them well,
who breaks the sluggish clods with mattocks
and over them drags his wicker hurdles;
golden Ceres does not look down on him
from high Olympus for nothing.

He too, who turns his plough aslant
and drives back through the ridges he raised
when first he cut the plain,
who works the earth without remission
and gives the fields their orders.

_Virgil, *Georgics* I_
