# West of the Pine Line

Cattle don’t come this way.  
Their path still cuts through  
  but no one’s walking it.

Whatever boundary  
  this was, _Pines that lie ahead_  
  even the grass ignores it now.

One jackrabbit stares.  
Still as stone.  
_Stone that lies ahead_  
Still.

The wind’s gone  
  to pick a fight somewhere else.  
Here,  
  even the dust  
    lies still.  
  _Stillness that lies ahead_

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 7/10**

> Abandoned cattle path, indifferent grass, vanished wind: boundary without bureaucrat.  
> Keep; reduce one “still,” preserving silence rather than naming it repeatedly.

**John Gould Fletcher — 8/10**

> The erased boundary and unused cattle path create a convincing western stillness beyond ownership.  
> Keep it, reducing the verbal insistence upon stillness so the empty ground may enact it.
