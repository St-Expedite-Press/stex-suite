# I. To A Pine's Finch

I saw you

A flake of flame in tumble reed-thatch  
You edge a branch on coiled steps

Paintbrush leans, new-split in blush,  
Milk-pink throat of the wild phlox.

The breeze bends back the brome’s green whip.  
Wings shake. Some heart forgets its grip.

I saw you  
and everything kept growing.

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 8/10**

> Flame in reed-thatch, milk-pink phlox, one released heart: clean seeing.  
> Keep; the modest ending understands growth better than the book’s louder revelations.

**John Gould Fletcher — 9/10**

> The bird’s small flame awakens an entire field without forcing nature into allegory.  
> Keep it untouched; its clear delight is essential among the darker transformations.
