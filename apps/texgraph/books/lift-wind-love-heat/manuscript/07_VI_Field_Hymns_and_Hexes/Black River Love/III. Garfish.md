# III. Garfish

Spite waters, scale-hacked, bleary,  
crowned with snarl, rough black armor,  
bends from oxbows under poisoned  
moonlight, Maytime—

Snouts through lilies, dragging heart-mud,  
splits green rot with a lurching  
grin, grimed with hunger older than rainfall,  
older than loving.

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 8/10**

> Armor, oxbow, heart-mud, ancient grin: the fish enters already made of consonants.  
> Keep; read aloud and alter nothing that still bites.

**John Gould Fletcher — 9/10**

> The creature rises from oxbow darkness in a compact surge of sound and primordial appetite.  
> Keep it; its rough consonantal movement completes the cycle’s descent from bird to rooted tree to armored water.
