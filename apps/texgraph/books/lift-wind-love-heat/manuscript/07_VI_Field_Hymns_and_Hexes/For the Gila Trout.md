# For the Gila Trout

_en los altos_

Living in the river,  
golden thrashing filament—  
she flexes in a shadow,  
a slip of fire  
flashing wet copper light.

She rises, flicks her flank—  
a ripple of gold  
unfastening the world.

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 8/10**

> Golden filament unfastens the world in ten clean lines.  
> Keep; no metaphysical invoice need follow the flash.

**John Gould Fletcher — 9/10**

> The trout is pure movement and copper light, briefly releasing the landscape from its fixed form.  
> Keep it exactly as a bright interval; its brevity is part of its freedom.
