# No Serrana

I have heard men  
compare women to spring.  
Loose habit.  
Dead speech.  
  
I asked  
_donde estaba?  
_Where is the cowherd girl?  
  
She smiled  
like someone hearing  
the wrong man  
knock on the right door.  
  
She said  
"that girl wants no part of love."  
Meaning herself._  
esa moza no quiere  
saber nada de amores.  
_  
She meant, in fact said:  
go back to your saddle.

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 8/10**

> The woman declines the inherited genre and sends its rider back to the saddle.  
> Keep; the broken italics need repair, not interpretation.

**John Gould Fletcher — 8/10**

> This concise refusal usefully overturns the courtly encounter and returns authority to the woman in the road.  
> Keep it, repairing the typography while preserving the dry force of her dismissal.
