# III. Forward Murder in a Reverse Day in Baton Rouge

At no other season does the lioness,
her cubs forgotten, range the plains more savage;
never do the shapeless bears deal death and havoc
so broadly through the woods. Then the boar
is murderous, then the tigress at her worst.

Bad, then, to travel alone
in Libya’s empty fields.

_Virgil, *Georgics* III_
