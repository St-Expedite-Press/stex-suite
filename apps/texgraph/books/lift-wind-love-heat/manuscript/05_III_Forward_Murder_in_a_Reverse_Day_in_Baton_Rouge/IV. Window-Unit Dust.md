# IV. Window-Unit Dust (late afternoon)

Must lift the wind, said the want-ad, paid by dust  
Sky chews an apple of rust  
A radiator  
  smelled  
    like meat  
**coupon-ghost glare** thick on the **_glass_**

_Lift the wind, love the heat_, the porch light pleads  
but the song spins backward and nobody stays

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 7/10**

> Rust-apple sky and backward song: brief, hot, serviceable.  
> Keep within the cycle; alone it would be insufficient, but sequence gives it force.

**John Gould Fletcher — 7/10**

> A brief panel of rust, heat, and vanishing music, effective chiefly through its place in the day’s reversal.  
> Retain it as connective tissue, but do not ask this fragment to bear an independent burden.
