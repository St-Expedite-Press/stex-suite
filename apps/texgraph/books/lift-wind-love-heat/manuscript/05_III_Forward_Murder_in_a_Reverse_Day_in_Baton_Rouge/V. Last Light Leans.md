# V. Last Light Leans (evening to night)

She comes in last / **coupon-ghost glare**  
Bleachluck folds grease into his frown  
Glasses they never owned riff minor tin  
jukebox needle rides the groove, slows down

Fan, one lazy wing  
Porch bulb chirps **STAY** in crooked Morse  
Shoes by the moon  
Spoon hollow letter brands the dark  
prayer boiled away

> **lift the wind / love the heat**

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 8/10**

> Grease, jukebox, spoon, failing Morse: aftermath compressed into household residue.  
> Keep; the murder remains indirect, but here indirection has weight.

**John Gould Fletcher — 8/10**

> The dying day settles into grease, fan, bulb, and minor music with a persuasive exhausted rhythm.  
> Keep it within the reversed sequence; its reticence gives the unseen violence an afterglow.
