# III. Noon, What They Don't Say

Don’t drink the tap, it sings off-key  
Wax drains, couch gone, fraud squats grinning  
He hones silence on his jaw  
Soup blues in the cracks

 Sky slides a hi-hat shimmer

**NEEDED:** one man who won’t come back  
Plays hoops, bleeds proud  
**Job: watch the kids and smoke government grass. Rent’s in the red. So is the baby.**  
Kids across the river

> **lift the wind / love the heat**

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 8/10**

> Want ad, government grass, red rent, red baby: public failure enters the room.  
> Keep; clarify “Kids across the river,” presently a gesture awaiting consequence.

**John Gould Fletcher — 8/10**

> Its clipped notices and overheated domestic details expose poverty without surrendering lyric pressure.  
> Keep it, clarifying the distant children so the river becomes more than a broken horizon.
