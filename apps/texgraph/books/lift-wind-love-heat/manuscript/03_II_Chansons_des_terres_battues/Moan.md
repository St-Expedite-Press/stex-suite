# Moan

Who said he fixed the ones who came too late  
his door was locked with thread and safety pins?  
his name was pissed in snow behind the store  
steaming melting erasing itself except for me...  
his icy glyph spewed no doubt with glee:  
_you want a reason  
start with salt_  
i knocked he didn’t speak but pulled a chair  
and traced a loop in air with one closed fist  
he waited then he pointed at my shoes  
they had snaky laces, unravelled knots of lust  
he turned and put a record on the wall  
it played a sound like wire cutting skin  
he didn’t nod he didn’t change his face  
i left before the light began to bend  
outside the sign read _try the one who howls

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 7/10**

> Safety pins, snow-piss, snaky laces: ugly matter brought properly into measure.  
> Keep; repair the ending so its incompletion feels inevitable rather than accidental.

**John Gould Fletcher — 7/10**

> The monologue carries a harsh, wintry folk surrealism whose objects retain their bodily sting.  
> Keep it, but give the unfinished sign a formal inevitability instead of the appearance of damaged transcription.
