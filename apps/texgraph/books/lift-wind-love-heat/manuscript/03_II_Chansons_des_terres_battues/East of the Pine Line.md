# East of the Pine Line

The heron struts  
 ain't got no lift.  
Watches the ditchwater  
 fold over the moon.

The porch light’s fixed now,  
 always on—  
 no blinking,  
  no mercy.

Even the wind  
knows better  
 than to knock.

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 7/10**

> Heron, ditchwater, fixed porch light: six strokes and the country stands.  
> Keep; it knows the value of stopping before explanation arrives.

**John Gould Fletcher — 8/10**

> A complete nocturne is made from ditchwater, porch light, and a wind wise enough to withdraw.  
> Keep it untouched; its restraint gives the movement a necessary region of silence.
