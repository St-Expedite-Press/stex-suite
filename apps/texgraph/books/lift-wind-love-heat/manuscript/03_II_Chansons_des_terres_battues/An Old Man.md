# An Old Man

And then the old man  
again...

Squirrels run  
 here and there—  
no pattern, no cover.

Stems do not bend.  
Tubers makes no motion.

Clouds drag a corner  
  across his back.  
Nothing displaces him.

One twig  
        snaps.  
Then two.  
Then (again) nothing at all.

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 6/10**

> Twig, squirrel, cloud-corner: precise particulars, but the old man remains only a post.  
> Keep if his stillness echoes later; otherwise the sketch has not paid passage.

**John Gould Fletcher — 6/10**

> The scene has the spare immobility of a landscape study, with life flickering around one fixed presence.  
> It may remain as a quiet interval, provided the old man’s stillness answers some later human burden.
