# To Myself, A Fevered Fool

_A Cameo From Catullus_

Says she’s, my woman, to wed no one.  
But me—not even if God himself come sniffing.  
She says this—what lovers pass on love-struck lips...  
Scratch it on wind and rushing water!

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 6/10**

> Catullus travels light, but this adaptation scarcely gets him off the platform.  
> Keep only if made sharper or paired visibly with another classical transformation.

**John Gould Fletcher — 5/10**

> The fragment has grace but remains chiefly an echo of its classical occasion rather than a new regional song.  
> Expand its local circumstance or omit it; allusion alone cannot establish a movement.
