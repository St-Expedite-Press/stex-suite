# Pairing Request

There was a night—  
  you kept talking  
about dish soap or stray cats,  
and I thought,  
  this is it.

> new device detected  
> would you like to remember this location?

No.  
    (yes.)  
        (always.)  
I held still.

You were just talking.  
I felt my own pulse in my armpit.

There was a night—  
  you looked at me  
  like the room was on fire.  
  Could I be a bucket?

I was saying something dumb  
 about cat hair and carpet cleaner.

> bluetooth pair request received  
> reject?  
> delay?  
> wait and see?

dumb  
about cat hair and carpet cleaner.

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 9/10**

> The machine’s little courtship rite strikes clean against cat hair and frightened flesh.  
> Keep nearly untouched; this is wit condensed until wit becomes ache.

**John Gould Fletcher — 9/10**

> Its electronic refrain becomes a delicate modern lyric of recognition, hesitation, and failed communion.  
> Keep it nearly as written; the ordinary talk gives the technological ghost its tenderness.
