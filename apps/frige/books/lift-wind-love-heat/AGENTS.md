# Lift Wind/Love Heat project instructions

These manuscript-specific rules supplement the [repository charter](../../../../AGENTS.md)
and [Frige charter](../../AGENTS.md). Engineering and release work must never
rewrite this project's protected manuscript as a fixture or migration shortcut.

This is a creative poetry manuscript. Work within `manuscript/`, `compiled/`, or `notes/` according to the requested artifact, retaining the existing section and cycle hierarchy.

- Do not relocate poems merely to make a generic chapter structure.
- Treat `compiled/` as a separate deliverable from the sectioned manuscript; reconcile intentional revisions rather than silently overwriting either.
- Keep source, editorial, and interpretive notes in `notes/` or an appropriate existing manuscript location.
- Do not apply the newsletter workflow or create a publish packet for this project.
