# VI. Field Hymns and Hexes

Olives, against all this,
 ask no cultivation.

They do not wait
 on the fore-curved hook
 or the grasping rakes,
once they have gripped their fields
 and stood up to the winds.

The earth herself,
 when the hooked tooth unlocks her,
gives moisture enough to the plants
 and heavy fruit,
 when the share unlocks her.

With the plough, then,
 feed the olive fat:

the tree
 that Peace has chosen.

_after Virgil, *Georgics* II_
