# Cold, Cold Again

It's getting cold again  
Damnit I thought I thought  
This was supposed to be over  
It's spring now and all the girls—  
What? Yes. It's spring now.  
Or it should be spring.  
  
Spring now and all the women  
Of New Orleans are starting  
Not to wear clothes, so this  
Should be over.  
  
But sitting there in the chill  
Two calico cats perfectly still  
Squatting on the window sill  
Under the banana leaves.  
  
I flew North too soon &  
You're there & it's  
       Getting  
            Cold  
               Again

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 5/10**

> The calico cats live; the undressed women are weather-report shorthand.  
> Rebuild around migration, window, and mistaken season—or cut without mourning.

**John Gould Fletcher — 6/10**

> The displaced season and motionless cats create a credible little song of geographical estrangement.  
> Revise the opening banter, whose casual generalization weakens the colder image waiting at the window.
