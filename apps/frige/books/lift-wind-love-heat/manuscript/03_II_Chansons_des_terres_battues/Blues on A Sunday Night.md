# Blues on A Sunday Night

rattle, rattle, rattle  
outside they rattle  
  
these cans  
the city won’t pick up  
  
that I put my trash in  
  
metal mouths  
knocking their own teeth out  
pretending they don’t care about  
  
wind shouldering thru our alley  
kicking their teeth against the curb  
  
rattle—  
  
something loose in them  
something

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 7/10**

> Trash cans knocking out their own teeth: one hard image carries the blues honestly.  
> Keep, but the final repetitions must change pressure rather than merely repeat sound.

**John Gould Fletcher — 7/10**

> The alley percussion is exact, and the rattling cans turn civic neglect into a broken refrain.  
> Retain it after shaping the closing repetitions into a more deliberate diminuendo.
