# The Kid Along The Frontage Road

_“The inwards of a fool are like a broken vessel.” —Ecclesiasticus 21:14_

But broken spills truth the sealed dare not taste,  
The Kid said that.

He found the pencil in the lining of his coat.  
No memory of packing it.  
Paper was torn from a ledger, stained with oil.

He wrote:  
**I was not born to end anything.**  
Then crossed it out.  
**I am not a name.**  
**I am not a game.**  
**I ain't tame.**  
Then: **I remember nothing of water.**

The sun was behind him.  
He could see the shape of his own head on the paper.

A noise entered him

let the mirror bend you & whistle in static & quiet vapor  
coils in & it can’t follow static & gather every vapor you  
can see but don’t break nothing** not that girl,  
she got a good family and she really tried & i

past all forms, be all drift! oh the hours with paint over their rust,  
the blueprint of keys & rain turned inside out—half-lit & inward  
with pulse & it flickers

—until you find seven worlds in the margin of a minute,  
some turning backwards, some without a sun,  
& have their own light  
behind seven closed doors.

He looked up.  
A Boy was walking toward him.  
_Who is the third who always_  
_walks_  
Thus spoke the noise

> **help mama! help those that cannot know not to understand  
> them who cain't understand not to know help them be young**  
> the cracker boy wears spiked shoes but his hands are  
> bare / the frenchman still dancing the cocktail tango...

He stood.  
Folded the paper.  
Put it in his boot.

The Boy raised the rifle.  
Said: “You’re just doing copies now.”

**Wild the doe sees not me.**  
**Pressed against the sky notsky**  
**It watches not the sky like me**

Then the wind howled:  
**“I’m not your mother, but I watched you sleep.”**  
Someone sang **“take off your shadow**"  
& The Kid thought  
"**& I SHALL LOOK DOWN"**  
the street now was full of men in borrowed hats.

& yes, they called it love  
when you couldn’t tell  
if the mirror was cracked  
or just remembering you differently.

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 10/10**

> Ledger paper, rifle-bearing Boy, borrowed hats, cracked remembering: the book returns as damaged signal.  
> Keep, but excise ten percent of the collage so the final mirror arrives as fate, not exhaustion.

**John Gould Fletcher — 9/10**

> The frontage road gathers the manuscript’s voices into a broken procession without pretending to resolve them.  
> Keep it as coda, thinning the collage so paper, Boy, wind, and final mirror remain audible within the tumult.
