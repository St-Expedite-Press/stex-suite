```
---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 5/10**

> The Latin scaffolding shows more strongly than the moon suspended upon it.  
> Condense to half; let one false consolation fail magnificently instead of four repeating theirs.

**John Gould Fletcher — 6/10**

> The alternating Latin and English create a ceremonial antiphony, though the lament circles without deepening.  
> Condense the ritual until moon, sleeper, and failed consolation form one continuous nocturnal movement.
   _quid restat? numquam deus exsomnis erit._
```

Why howl you, Moon?  
Hair undone, dry-eyed?  
What is burning through your nights?

```
   _sed miror, qualis numinis umbra foret._
```

Your hunter sleeps, his skin  
pale cut ivory, set  
into the sky like some Eastern god.

```
  _at deus, exanimes numquam vidisset honores._
```

Better, then! Let sleep hammer shut  
your thoughts, press them to stone.  
The world turns without you,  
and at day’s edge,  
the hour will soften, relent.

```
    _et numen extinctum somnia nosse potest._
```
