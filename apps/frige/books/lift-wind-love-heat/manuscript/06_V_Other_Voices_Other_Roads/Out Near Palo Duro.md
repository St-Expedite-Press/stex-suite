# Out Near Palo Duro

**I**

Never was a girl that rode fences  
Nor ever cut the wind  
like the _hacendado's_ daughter  
out near Palo Duro.

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 6/10**

> Good ballad hoofbeat, but the hacendado’s daughter remains a heraldic figure.  
> Give her one action not designed for admiration, or let this song ride out.

**John Gould Fletcher — 6/10**

> The ballad measure carries the plains attractively, but the admired rider remains part of the scenery.  
> Retain only if she is granted an action or voice that breaks the ornamental convention.

**II**

Riding there was I, dead West  
out from old Mission Candela;  
tracking that shrine  
called Saint Rosalia Mesa.  
In sleep I dropped the bridle,  
Took flight from sense and saddle.  
Fell I clean into her quarter  
out near Palo Duro.

**III**

Pastures sang with gramma  
and prairiefire bloomed.  
She moved her colt in silence,  
longhorns shifting room.  
I saw her plain. No manner  
of lady born in manor  
could match the _hacendado's_ daughter  
out near Palo Duro.
