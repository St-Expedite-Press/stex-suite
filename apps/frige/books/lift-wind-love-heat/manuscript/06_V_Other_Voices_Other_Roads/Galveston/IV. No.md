### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 8/10**

> One word closes three poems of bargaining: exact and cruel.  
> Keep only as the cycle’s fourth movement; typography and page placement must carry it.

**John Gould Fletcher — 8/10**

> As the terminal beat of the cycle, the word falls with the finality of a shut Gulf door.  
> Retain it only in sequence and give its white space the dignity of silence.
