# Harmonikum

There is a turning I watch alone, of worlds spinning
Around unseen threads, motion eternal, vast, unbreaking
Seven voices rising in song, Seven echoes gleaming  
Each reflecting the next, circles of sound and light.

Far beyond, distance softens the blaze of Heavens,  
Phoebus presses one note into the dark.  
The gods wait—still, serene, unheard.  
And in the void between the spheres,  
He dreams of music once again.

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 5/10**

> Too much Heaven, Phoebus, golden echo: the celestial furniture arrives already polished.  
> Either dent these spheres in the poem itself or begin the movement with Venus.

**John Gould Fletcher — 6/10**

> Its planetary music establishes the intended symphonic scale, though the light remains generalized and remote.  
> Retain only if the diction is roughened until the cosmic harmony bears the manuscript’s mortal weather.
