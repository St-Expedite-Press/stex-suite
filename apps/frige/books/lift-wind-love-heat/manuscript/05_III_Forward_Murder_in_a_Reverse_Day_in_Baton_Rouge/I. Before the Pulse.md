# I. Before the Pulse (origins)

Orient marauders whisper code  
To a queen unborn  
A telegram arrives: {STOP.  
IT’S THE SHERIFF’S LUNGS.  
THEY’VE BEEN TRANSLATED INTO SPANISH.  
STOP}  
Blueprint jails quake

> **lift the wind**

None of this happened  
{THROW THIS AWAY}  
When the band gets here  
All of it will

> **love the heat**

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 9/10**

> Telegram lungs translated into Spanish: the crime begins as impossible information.  
> Keep; the title’s buried phrase earns its recurrence and the book’s name.

**John Gould Fletcher — 8/10**

> The collage has prophetic velocity, making language itself the unstable origin of the coming violence.  
> Keep it, though one command might be removed so the two title phrases ring more starkly.
