# II. Fridge Buzz (pre-dawn to dawn)

**Their Fridge buzzing  
Like light dying in a FEMA trailer**  
Eggs long haloed green  
Knives off-kilter  
Dream screens snow-red static

Faucet mutters _let it be_

Dog yelps time  
Sink ticks a rim-shot jaw  
Coffee hisses  
Her neck raw to touch

 Violet sky, count-in before the down beat

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 8/10**

> FEMA trailer, green eggs, knife, raw neck: the objects accuse without sermon.  
> Keep; this is the cycle’s most disciplined pressure chamber.

**John Gould Fletcher — 9/10**

> The refrigerator’s mechanical drone orchestrates a whole damaged household before dawn.  
> Keep it; each object bears social history while the violet sky supplies the necessary release.
