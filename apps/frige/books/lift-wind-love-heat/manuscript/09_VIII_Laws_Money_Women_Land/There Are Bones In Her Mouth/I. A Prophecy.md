# I. A Prophecy

Momma Magdalene opens the birdcage.  
Her radio plays only the weather—  
static and barometric pressure,  
droning like the Scripture.

She wipes her mouth with a curtain.  
Her breath:  
salted iron,  
Windex,  
Coors can skunk.

She lights another cigarette  
off the stovetop burner  
and says:  
"Whichever you pick,  
them dogs’ll find your bones."

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 9/10**

> Windex, Coors, stovetop flame, barometric scripture: the prophet has a kitchen.  
> Keep untouched; fifteen lines establish more world than several full movements.

**John Gould Fletcher — 10/10**

> The kitchen prophet is wholly alive in smell, weather, gesture, and the hard economy of her warning.  
> Keep it untouched; its local objects have already become myth without surrendering their dirt.
