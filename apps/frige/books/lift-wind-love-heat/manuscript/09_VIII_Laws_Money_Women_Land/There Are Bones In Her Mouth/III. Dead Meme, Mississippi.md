# III. Dead Meme, Mississippi

Haints or something  
Got my dream last night.  
I found him face down in a ditch  
off County Highway 4,  
half-eaten by ants  
and wrapped in a Garfield blanket  
that said _MONDAY HURTS_ in purple.

**you laugh / you scroll**

They’d tied a vape cartridge  
to his collar  
and stuffed his mouth  
with a chicken receipt  
dated six months ago.

**you laugh / scroll**

You got the witch.

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 7/10**

> Garfield blanket and vape cartridge make digital rot briefly physical.  
> Keep provisionally; reduce the visual “scroll” performance and trust the corpse’s absurd particulars.

**John Gould Fletcher — 7/10**

> The grotesque meeting of roadside death and discarded digital comedy is memorable, though the visual refrain overstates itself.  
> Keep after shortening the scrolling chorus so Garfield blanket, receipt, and ditch retain their dreadful comedy.
