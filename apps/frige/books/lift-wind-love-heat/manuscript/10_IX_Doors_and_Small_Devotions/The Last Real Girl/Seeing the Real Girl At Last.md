# Seeing the Real Girl At Last

With no child between them  
a perfect score of z-e-r-o,  
two lovers wrapped in hotel covers  
are trying desperately to remember  
"Did we existed before television?"  
"Are we just fucking xovers?"

Above their heads  
billboard lights flicker weakly:

NO FUTURE REFUNDS  
NO FUTURE REFUNDS

By midnight. Fuck it's late midnight? By midnight  
even the local moon looks dipped in radiator fluid.

UNTIL FURTHER NOTICE  
THE SUN HAS BEEN REPLACED  
BY A MORE AFFORDABLE SUN

And still, a river of halogen headlamps;  
the highways keep rolling West.

---

### Editorial Includability

**Ezra Pound — 5/10**

> The cheaper sun and refund billboard are sharp; the opening errors blunt their edge.  
> Rebuild the first stanza, preserve the westbound halogen river, and decide whether midnight’s stumble is deliberate.

**John Gould Fletcher — 6/10**

> The commercial sky and endless headlamps create a bleakly effective modern nocturne after intimacy has failed.  
> Rebuild the opening syntax, retaining the cheaper sun, refund sign, radiator moon, and westward flow.
