# Preface

Go, little one  
touch, where I may not, the dear places  
at least, in you, I reach them.

  If there is yet one among the throng who recalls me,  
(who has carried my name through strange waters,  
returned with words sown far away),  
Let him look upon you,  
let him read  
or, if he must, scorn you.

  If there be such a fool  
that he should fling you aside...  
Let him!  
Let another call you wretched.

  Meanwhile  
little bird, fate still suffers it  
let us entwine, you & I.  
Soon enough, death shall come,  
head wrapped in black.
